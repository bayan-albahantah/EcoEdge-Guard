EcoEdge-Guard: Smart Marine Buoy System

-EcoEdge-Guard is a smart early detection system designed to protect coastal environments, coral reefs, and fisheries using edge AI and hybrid satellite-LoRaWAN telemetry.

System Overview

-By deploying smart buoys in coastal and offshore waters, the system monitors marine environments in real time and predicts hazards such as Red Tide, marine pollution, and coral bleaching before they occur.

Working Principle
1. Data Collection: Ocean parameters including water temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, salinity, turbidity, chlorophyll-a, ammonia, and nitrate are automatically detected in real time.
2. Edge Analysis: On-site sensor data is processed locally using Edge AI models on integrated microcontrollers (ESP32).
3. Early Warning Transmission: When hazards are detected, urgent alerts are sent immediately to the central control dashboard via hybrid LoRaWAN and satellite IoT networks.

Hardware and Components

-Microcontroller Unit: ESP32 for local processing and anomaly detection.
-Water Quality & Coral Health Suite: Sensors measuring temperature, pH, salinity, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, chlorophyll-a, ammonia, and nitrate to monitor water safety and track conditions leading to coral bleaching.
-Power System: Solar panels and rechargeable marine batteries for continuous operation.
-Mechanical Build: High-density polyethylene (HDPE) buoys and 316L marine-grade stainless steel for structural strength in marine environments.
-Protection and Mooring: IP68 waterproof housing, heavy mooring chains, and secure anchors.

 
Network and Telemetry

-Hybrid Telemetry: Nearshore LoRaWAN gateways combined with deep-sea satellite transceivers ensure continuous coverage without signal loss.
-Target Operators: Environmental authorities, coast guards, ports, and offshore energy companies.

Impact

-Environmental Protection: Prevents ecological disasters, tracks marine health, and protects coral reefs through real-time monitoring.
-Safety and Economy: Provides satellite emergency messaging for fishermen and supports sustainable marine activities.
